### AI Engineer

**About Us**
We are building next-generation AI-powered commerce infrastructure and intelligent platforms that help businesses become AI-agent ready. Our products combine LLMs, data pipelines, automation systems, agentic workflows, and scalable cloud infrastructure to solve real-world commerce and enterprise problems.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Design, develop, and deploy scalable AI/ML systems, intelligent automation pipelines, and production-grade AI services.
- Architect and optimize LLM-powered applications, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, AI agents, multi-agent workflows, and orchestration frameworks.
- Develop robust backend services and APIs using Python, Node.js, and TypeScript to support AI-driven applications.
- Integrate and manage enterprise AI models and platforms such as OpenAI, Claude, Grok, Llama, and vector database technologies.
- Build and maintain data pipelines, embedding workflows, semantic search capabilities, recommendation engines, and knowledge retrieval systems.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and design teams to deliver scalable AI-first products and features.
- Design, evaluate, and optimize prompts, model outputs, guardrails, and inference workflows to improve system accuracy, reliability, and performance.
- Implement scalable cloud-native architectures leveraging distributed systems, message queues, databases, caching layers, and containerized infrastructure.
- Contribute to system architecture, technical design reviews, code quality standards, testing strategies, and engineering documentation.
- Monitor advancements in AI/ML technologies, emerging protocols, open-source ecosystems, and industry best practices to drive continuous innovation.

**Required Skills**

- 4–7 years of software engineering experience with strong backend development expertise.
- Hands-on experience with AI/ML systems and LLM-based applications.

**Strong proficiency in:**

- Python and/or Node.js
- TypeScript/JavaScript
- REST APIs & GraphQL
- PostgreSQL / MongoDB / Redis

**Experience with:**

- OpenAI, Claude, Llama, or similar LLMs
- RAG pipelines and vector databases
- LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, or agent frameworks
- Cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure)
- Docker & CI/CD workflows
- Understanding of scalable system design and distributed architectures
- Strong problem-solving and debugging skills

**Good to Have**

- Experience with Shopify ecosystem or eCommerce platforms.
- Experience building AI agents or autonomous workflows.
- Knowledge of Playwright, scraping, or crawler systems.
- Exposure to NLP, embeddings, and semantic search.
- Familiarity with Kubernetes and microservices architecture.

**What We Offer**

- Opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI products.
- High ownership and fast-paced environment.
- Flexible work culture.
- Competitive salary and growth opportunities.
- Exposure to real-world AI product deployment at scale.
